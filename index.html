    How the model was built

    Walls and floors are traced from the brochure's vector plan and scaled with its own 0–10 m bar ( pt per metre). Areas in m². Room height m, door heads 2.10 m and window sills 0.90 m are assumptions, since the plan gives no heights. Door leaves are left out so openings stay readable.

    Where furniture sizes come from

    This page can't open ikea.com itself, so it reads the product name and article number from the link. Sizes come from a built-in list of common IKEA products checked against IKEA's published measurements. Products not on the list get an estimate from Claude, which can't browse either, or a typical size for the shape. Estimates are marked. Check them against the IKEA product page and correct the numbers before you rely on a fit.
